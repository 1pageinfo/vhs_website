  • What is Steel Detailing?
    Steel detailing involves creating detailed fabrication drawings and specifications for steel structures, ensuring accurate manufacturing, assembly, and erection of steel components.
  • Why is Steel Detailing Important?
    Steel detailing ensures precision in fabrication, reduces material waste, minimizes errors during construction, and ensures compliance with safety and building codes.
  • What Software Do You Use for Steel Detailing?
    We use industry-standard software such as AutoCAD, Tekla Structures, and SDS/2 for creating detailed steel fabrication drawings and models.
  • What Types of Projects Do You Handle?
    We handle steel detailing for high-rise buildings, industrial plants, warehouses, bridges, offshore structures, and architectural elements like facades and staircases.
  • How Long Does Steel Detailing Take?
    The timeline depends on project complexity, but typically ranges from a few weeks to months. We prioritize efficiency to meet your construction schedules.
  • Do You Provide CNC Files?
    Yes, we provide CNC-ready files for automated cutting, drilling, and fabrication, ensuring precision and reducing manual labor.
