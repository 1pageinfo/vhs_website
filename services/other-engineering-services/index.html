    About Other Engineering Services

    Our specialized engineering services supplement core structural and detailing work, delivering accurate CAD conversion, disciplined drafting, and clear fabrication planning.

    From as-built conversions to erection methodology, we help clients reduce waste, improve coordination, and execute projects with confidence.

    Why Choose Our Other Engineering Services Services

    VHS Engineering provides complementary engineering services that streamline planning and execution. Our support helps projects move more efficiently, from drawings to erection.
