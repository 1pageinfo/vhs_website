Connection Engineering

About Connection Design Services

Our connection design services deliver safe, code-compliant, and fabrication-ready connection solutions for steel structures, metal stairs, and architectural steel components.

We provide sealed calculation packages, field repair details, and practical sketches that support effective approval, fabrication, and erection.

Why Choose Our Connection Design Services Services

VHS Engineering focuses on connection performance with precision engineering, clear documentation, and code compliance. Our connections are designed for strength, constructability, and ease of installation.
