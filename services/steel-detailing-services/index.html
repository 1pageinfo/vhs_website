Steel Detailing

About Steel Detailing Services

Our steel detailing services produce fabrication-ready shop drawings for buildings, industrial facilities, bridges, and architectural steel elements with exacting accuracy.

We coordinate structural and architectural steel, stairs, rails, ductwork, and plate assemblies to ensure efficient fabrication and site installation.

Why Choose Our Steel Detailing Services Services

VHS Engineering delivers steel detailing that balances precision and practicality. Our drawings support fabrication, installation, and compliance with global standards for safer, faster execution.
