About Estimodelling & Pre-Bid Services

We believe accurate planning at the earliest stage reduces risks, optimizes costs, and enhances bid competitiveness. Our Estimodelling & Pre-Bid Services provide clarity and strategic advantage even before execution begins.

At VHS Engineering, we offer comprehensive estimodeling and pre-bid solutions including 3D conceptual modeling, material takeoffs, cost estimations, and bid preparation to ensure precise project planning and successful tendering.

Why Choose Our Estimodelling & Pre-Bid Services

VHS Engineering's estimodeling and pre-bid expertise ensures accurate quantity takeoffs, cost estimations, and risk assessments, enabling clients to submit competitive bids with confidence. Our detailed BOQ preparations and value engineering save time and costs.

01
Estimodelling Services 3D Conceptual Modelling, Material Take-Offs, Weight Calculations, and early clash identification for reliable procurement and costing.
02
Pre-Bid Services BOQ Preparation, Value Engineering, Bid Package Support, and risk identification for competitive and accurate bidding.
03
Quantity Takeoff Accurate extraction of quantities from drawings for precise budgeting and logistics planning.
04
Cost Estimation Detailed cost breakdowns and alternative design options to optimize project economics.

Key Offerings

  • Estimodelling Services: 3D Conceptual Modelling (LOD 100–200) for feasibility & planning. Material Take-Offs (MTO) & Quantity Extraction for reliable procurement and costing. Weight Calculations (Steel, Bolts, Welds) for accurate budgeting & logistics. Preliminary Connection Assumptions for fabrication/erection forecasting. Early Clash Identification to prevent rework and delays. Basic GA Drawings to validate material quantities and provide visual clarity.
  • Pre-Bid Services: BOQ Preparation – Comprehensive Bill of Quantities. Value Engineering – Alternatives that save time, labour, and cost. Bid Package Support – Drawings, reports, and markups for professional submissions. Risk Identification – Early detection of high-cost or complex design areas. Alternative Design Options – Practical solutions to accelerate schedules and reduce costs.
  • What is estimodeling?
    Estimodeling involves creating 3D models and extracting quantities for accurate cost estimation and planning before detailed design, helping to identify risks and optimize budgets early.
  • How do pre-bid services help in bidding?
    Pre-bid services provide detailed BOQs, value engineering options, and risk assessments, enabling accurate bid preparation and increasing competitiveness.
  • What is a Bill of Quantities (BOQ)?
    A BOQ is a comprehensive document listing all materials, quantities, and costs required for a project, used for accurate pricing and procurement.
  • How does value engineering reduce costs?
    Value engineering identifies alternative materials or methods that maintain quality while reducing costs, labor, or time without compromising functionality.
  • What tools do you use for estimodeling?
    We use software like Tekla, Revit, and Excel for 3D modeling, quantity extraction, and cost analysis to ensure precision and efficiency.
  • Why is early estimation important?
    Early estimation reduces risks, allows for better planning, and helps in making informed decisions to optimize costs and timelines before construction begins.
