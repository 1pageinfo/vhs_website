  • What is connection design?
    Connection design involves calculating and detailing how structural elements like beams and columns are joined, ensuring safety, stability, and compliance with building codes.
  • Which codes do you follow for connection design?
    We follow international standards such as AISC (American), CSA (Canadian), IS (Indian), and BS (British) codes to ensure code-compliant and safe connections.
  • What is included in metal stair design?
    Metal stair design includes detailing stringers, treads, handrails, and connections to ensure structural integrity, safety, and compliance with regulations.
  • Do you provide field repair details?
    Yes, we offer detailed field repair and redesign support for on-site modifications, ensuring quick and safe resolutions to structural issues.
  • What are sealed calculation books?
    Sealed calculation books are official documents prepared by certified engineers, providing detailed calculations and submissions for regulatory approval.
  • Why is connection design important?
    Proper connection design ensures the structural integrity of buildings, prevents failures, and guarantees safety under various loads and conditions.
